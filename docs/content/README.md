# Department content drafts

Draft content for the per-department version of CS Career: each department has tracks, a 12-question track quiz and one full sample track page. Once lecturers approve them, these become the `departments`, `tracks` and `questions` data in MongoDB, editable from the admin.

| Artificial Intelligence | [ai-draft.md](ai-draft.md) | 6 | Generative AI & Agents |
| Cybersecurity | [cy-draft.md](cy-draft.md) | 7 | SOC & Blue Team |
| Internet of Things | [iot-draft.md](iot-draft.md) | 6 | Edge AI & TinyML |

**Scoring and starting level:** defined once in [se-draft.md](se-draft.md), section 2, and used by every department.

**Every track page has the same 8 sections:** what it is · a day in the job · how AI is changing this job · roadmap (Beginner, Intermediate, Job-ready) · projects to build · where to learn (free, then paid) · certifications · careers · related tracks.

## Review checklist
1. A lecturer from each department approves the track list.
2. Try each quiz with 10–15 students already in that department and check the results feel right; adjust weights if not.
3. Open every resource link once before publishing.
4. Translate to Somali and Arabic, then have native speakers proofread.
5. Set "Last reviewed" to the review date.
